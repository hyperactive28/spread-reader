Spread

A manga reader designed for iPhone Duo, iPhone and iPad.
HomePrivacySupport

Spread reads the manga you already own — CBZ, CBR and PDF — with a library that understands chapters and volumes, iCloud sync, and a reading experience built for two-page spreads on iPhone Duo.

  • Chapters grouped into volumes, with what's missing shown at a glance
  • Page curl, two-page spreads and right-to-left reading
  • Privacy mode that hides covers and titles
  • Reading progress synced across your devices with iCloud

Spread Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription and includes Family Sharing.
