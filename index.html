Reading that fits your screen

Spread adapts to iPhone Duo’s compact and unfolded layouts. Choose single pages, two-page spreads or continuous scrolling, with reading controls suited to the space available.

Your comics, your way

Read CBZ, CBR and PDF on iPhone and iPad, or try the included offline Pepper & Carrot sample. Import files and folders from Files or transfer them over local Wi-Fi with a PIN.

  • Single pages, two-page spreads and continuous scrolling, with right-to-left reading
  • Thumbnail navigation, bookmarks, crop, wide-page options and Lock Zoom
  • A library organized by chapters and volumes, with optional metadata matching
  • Local storage or iCloud file sync, plus reading-progress and status sync

Pick up your next chapter

Add Continue Reading, Reading Progress and optional Cover Gallery widgets. Use Handoff to continue on another device where the same comic is available, or use Shortcuts to open your library and start reading. iOS controls widget refresh; remove existing personal widgets before sharing a device with a child. See setup and privacy guidance.

14 days of Spread Pro

Every new reader gets 14 days of Pro with no account, sign-up or purchase needed. Nothing is charged when the free period ends. Reading and library tools stay free, including progress sync and up to 15 active iCloud comic files.

Monthly or yearly Pro subscriptions include Family Sharing, page curl, privacy covers and hidden titles, unlimited iCloud file sync, dynamic backgrounds, comic locks, image adjustments and alternate app icons. Subscribing during the free period starts billing immediately. Prices appear in the app for your region; manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Made in Australia. No advertising or tracking. Spread’s public App Store release is being prepared.
