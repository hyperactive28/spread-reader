Reading on iPhone Duo

How does reading adapt when I open or close Duo? Spread adapts its reader and controls to the available screen. Use Reader Settings → Layout to choose single pages, two-page spreads or continuous scrolling, and your preferred reading direction.

Common questions

Which files does Spread read? CBZ, CBR and PDF.

A volume says “On another device”. Its library entry has synced, but the comic file is not available here yet. Import the file on this device, or make it available through iCloud Drive. For manual additions, use the Spread folder or an Inbox folder; Spread Library is managed by the app. Apple transfers files when a connection and iCloud storage are available.

How do I organize my iCloud folders? Update Spread on all your devices first. In Settings → iCloud & Sync, choose Organize iCloud Library. Eligible managed files move into Spread → Spread Library → title → chapter files, with reading positions, groups and bookmarks retained and verified device backups kept. Folders you created stay where they are. You can retry interrupted organization from the same screen. Enable it on each updated device for future imports.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription? Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Does Spread Pro work for my family? Yes — Spread Pro includes Family Sharing for up to six family members at no extra cost.

How do I restore my purchase? In Spread, open Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases.

Parental controls

How do I set up child access? Open Settings → Parental Controls, create a separate six-digit parent PIN and save the recovery code somewhere your child cannot access. Choose the permitted content bands, then use Review Blocked and Unrated Comics to approve individual files. General is the default permitted band; unrated comics require individual approval. Choose End Parent Session before handing over the device. Parent access also ends when Spread goes into the background.

Does the device passcode unlock parent settings? No. The parent PIN is separate from device unlock and from Spread Pro comic locks. Parental controls are free and apply only in Spread on this device. Configure each device separately. Source ratings can be incomplete or inaccurate and do not analyse pages; review the content yourself. These controls do not replace Screen Time or restrict other apps.

I forgot my parent PIN. In Parental Controls, choose Forgot Parent PIN? and use your saved recovery code to set a new PIN. Spread does not keep a readable copy of either secret, and the developer cannot retrieve them. After too many failed attempts, wait five minutes before trying again. Save the recovery code during setup; do not rely on your device passcode as a fallback.

Why does an approved comic need approval again? Approvals apply to that file’s contents on this device and account. Replaced files and changed ratings require a new approval. Changing the iCloud account clears individual approvals. Download a comic to this device before approving it.

Widgets, StandBy and Handoff

How do I add widgets? Touch and hold the Home Screen, choose Edit → Add Widget, then search for Spread. Continue Reading and Reading Progress show eligible reading information. In Spread’s Settings → Appearance → Widgets & StandBy, optionally choose up to six covers for Cover Gallery. Small widgets can also appear in StandBy. iOS controls refresh and rotation timing, so changes may not appear immediately.

Why is a widget neutral? Privacy Mode, parental protection or an iCloud account change can prevent personal content from appearing. Locked comics are excluded. Parental protection keeps new widget content neutral even during a parent session. iOS may keep earlier widget images, so remove existing personal widgets before giving a device to a child. Selecting a widget always checks access again in Spread.

How does Handoff continue reading? The receiving device needs the same library item and its comic file available. Handoff passes the reading position, not the comic file. Personal Handoff is unavailable while Privacy Mode or parental protection is enabled, or for locked comics. After changing iCloud accounts, review your retained library in Settings → Appearance → Widgets & StandBy before choosing Resume Widgets, Handoff & Comic Links. Turn off Privacy Mode and parental protection to resume; choose gallery covers again separately.

Cover exports

Can Spread change my wallpaper? In the reader toolbar, choose Export Cover, then Save or Share Cover. This exports the comic’s physical first page. Save the image to Photos, then use iOS Settings → Wallpaper → Add New Wallpaper → Photo Shuffle to choose saved covers. Spread does not automatically change wallpaper or control shuffle timing. Export is unavailable for locked comics or while Privacy Mode or parental protection is enabled.

Will changing privacy settings remove exported images? No. Copies saved to Photos, Files or another app remain there. Spread cannot recall them; manage those copies in the destination app.
