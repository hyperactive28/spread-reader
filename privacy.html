Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Spread is built so that your library stays yours. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Nothing. Spread contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and the developer receives no personal information about you or what you read.

Your files and reading data

The comics and manga you import, your reading progress, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own iCloud account by Apple and synced between your devices. The developer cannot access your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Upload over Wi-Fi

When you start Upload over Wi-Fi, Spread runs a local upload page for devices on the same Wi-Fi network. A four-digit PIN shown in Spread is required to send a file. Files transfer directly to your device and are stored there; they do not pass through the developer's servers. The browser can also manage your library and edit its details. Private comics are hidden by default; you can show them in that browser after connecting with the PIN. You can continue transfers while reading inside Spread. Transfers stop when you stop the server, lock the device or switch to another app. Storage sharing is off by default. If you enable Show storage in browser under Settings > Wi-Fi Server, storage figures are shared only over the local network for display on your other device. This choice is remembered until you turn it off. Deleting an uploaded comic also removes its uploaded copy from that device.

Library statistics and preferences

Genre charts are calculated on your device from metadata already in your library. They are not sent to the developer or an analytics service. Locked comics are excluded. Title-language, content-rating, text-size and date-format choices are stored on your device.

Automatic metadata and optional connections

Spread can look up titles, creators, ISBNs, issue or volume numbers, language and publication years using Kitsu and Open Library. These requests help find descriptions, covers and publication details. Your comic files, page images, local folder paths, reading progress and bookmarks are not uploaded. Sources receive the search terms and ordinary connection information such as your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. You can disable Automatic Metadata and choose enabled sources under Settings > Library & Metadata > Metadata Sources.

You can optionally connect your own Metron account using an API token to find missing comic issue information. Spread stores that token in this device’s Keychain and sends it only to Metron for authentication. It does not request your provider password. Tokens are not synced to iCloud or included in sidecar metadata or exports. Disconnecting removes the token and cancels pending lookups; previously downloaded metadata remains in your library. Provider attributions are retained with metadata.

On supported devices, Spread may use Apple’s on-device language model to extract a search title from a difficult filename. This optional assistance has no web access, sends no filename to an AI server and falls back to ordinary parsing when unavailable. It does not read comic pages or invent catalogue information.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

Spread does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
