Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Spread is built so that your library stays yours. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't track you.

What we collect

Nothing. Spread contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and the developer receives no personal information about you or what you read.

Your files and reading data

The comics and manga you import, your reading progress, bookmarks and settings are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are stored in your own iCloud account by Apple and synced between your devices. The developer cannot access your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Series information (Kitsu)

To show covers, synopses and volume information, Spread may look up the titles of series in your library using the public Kitsu database. Only the series title or Kitsu ID is sent; no personal information is included. Kitsu's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Children

Spread does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions? See Support.
